About us

Built by people who know the operating theatre

Listermedica Healthcare was started in 2018 by a team with years of experience in medical and surgical work. Our aim has been simple from the start: make disposable drapes, packs and gowns to the highest standard.

Our quality system is certified to ISO 13485, and we are licensed by the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation as a medical device manufacturer. A wide business network across Kerala means we can support hospitals with dependable, regular supply.
